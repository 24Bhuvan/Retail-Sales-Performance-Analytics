# Purpose of this Document

The purpose of this document is to identify the key business stakeholders who would typically participate in a retail analytics project and to understand their responsibilities, objectives, and information needs.

Understanding stakeholder expectations ensures that future analytical work remains aligned with business priorities and supports effective decision-making.

# Stakeholder Profiles

## 1. Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

### Primary Responsibility

Provide strategic direction and monitor overall business performance.

### Business Objectives

* Monitor company growth.
* Evaluate sales performance across regions.
* Identify high-performing product categories.
* Support strategic business decisions using reliable data.

### Information Required

* Overall sales trends.
* Regional sales performance.
* Customer growth.
* Executive performance dashboard.

---

## 2. Sales Manager

### Primary Responsibility

Monitor sales performance and improve revenue generation.

### Business Objectives

* Identify top-selling products.
* Compare category performance.
* Monitor monthly sales trends.
* Identify underperforming product categories.

### Information Required

* Sales by category.
* Sales by state.
* Monthly revenue.
* Order volume.

---

## 3. Marketing Manager

### Primary Responsibility

Support marketing decisions using customer and product insights.

### Business Objectives

* Identify popular products.
* Understand customer purchasing behavior.
* Recognize seasonal purchasing patterns.
* Support promotional planning.

### Information Required

* Best-selling products.
* Product category demand.
* Customer purchasing trends.
* Geographic customer distribution.

---

## 4. Operations Manager

### Primary Responsibility

Monitor operational efficiency across order fulfillment and delivery.

### Business Objectives

* Improve delivery performance.
* Identify delayed deliveries.
* Evaluate seller performance.
* Monitor order fulfillment efficiency.

### Information Required

* Average delivery time.
* Delivery delays.
* Seller performance.
* Order status distribution.

---

## 5. Customer Experience Manager

### Primary Responsibility

Improve customer satisfaction and overall shopping experience.

### Business Objectives

* Monitor customer feedback.
* Identify causes of poor ratings.
* Improve service quality.
* Track customer satisfaction trends.

### Information Required

* Review scores.
* Review distribution.
* Delivery performance versus customer ratings.
* Customer feedback analysis.

---

## 6. Business Intelligence Team

### Primary Responsibility

Develop analytical reports and executive dashboards.

### Business Objectives

* Consolidate data from multiple sources.
* Build reliable business reports.
* Monitor organizational KPIs.
* Deliver interactive dashboards.

### Information Required

* Clean integrated datasets.
* Business metrics.
* Historical trends.
* Executive reporting requirements.

# Stakeholder Expectations (Simulated)

Based on the simulated business scenario, stakeholders expect the analytics solution to:

* Provide accurate and reliable business reporting.
* Consolidate multiple datasets into a unified analytical model.
* Deliver interactive dashboards for decision-making.
* Support monitoring of business performance over time.
* Enable self-service access to key business metrics.
* Reduce manual reporting effort through automated analytics.

---

# Stakeholder Challenges (Simulated)

The following challenges are assumed as part of the simulated business environment:

* Business data is distributed across multiple datasets.
* Reporting requires significant manual effort.
* Limited visibility into overall business performance.
* Difficulty identifying sales trends and operational bottlenecks.
* Lack of centralized business intelligence reporting.
