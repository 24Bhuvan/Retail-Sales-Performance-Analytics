# Executive Summary

The Retail Sales Performance Analytics project aims to develop an end-to-end business intelligence solution using the Olist Brazilian E-commerce Dataset. The project follows the CRISP-DM methodology and demonstrates the complete lifecycle of a modern data analytics project, beginning with business understanding and concluding with executive reporting.

The solution will integrate multiple relational datasets, perform data preparation and analysis, define business KPIs, and deliver interactive Power BI dashboards to support data-driven decision-making.

---

# Business Justification

Retail organizations generate large volumes of transactional data through customers, orders, products, sellers, payments, and delivery operations. While this information contains valuable business insights, it is often distributed across multiple datasets, making comprehensive analysis difficult.

A centralized analytics solution enables business leaders to monitor organizational performance, identify operational trends, evaluate customer behavior, and support strategic planning through reliable reporting and visualization.

---

# Project Objectives

The project aims to:

* Develop an integrated analytical dataset from multiple relational tables.
* Perform comprehensive retail sales analysis.
* Measure key business performance indicators.
* Build interactive dashboards for business users.
* Generate actionable business insights.
* Demonstrate an end-to-end analytics workflow using industry-standard tools.

# Project Scope

This project includes the complete analytics lifecycle, including business understanding, data preparation, SQL analysis, exploratory data analysis, KPI development, dashboard creation, business insight generation, and documentation.

Activities such as inventory optimization, predictive modeling, marketing attribution, customer lifetime value prediction, and production deployment are outside the scope of this project.

# Assumptions

* The Olist dataset represents historical retail transactions.
* Dataset relationships are sufficient for analytical reporting.
* Business objectives are simulated for portfolio purposes.
* Historical data is suitable for descriptive analytics.

---

# Constraints

* Only publicly available Olist data is used.
* Product cost and profit data are unavailable.
* Inventory information is unavailable.
* Marketing campaign data is unavailable.
* No real stakeholder engagement exists.

# Project Risks

| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
