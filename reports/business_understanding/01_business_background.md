# Company Overview (Simulated)

For this project, Olist is treated as a nationwide Brazilian e-commerce marketplace that connects customers with thousands of independent sellers through a centralized online platform. The marketplace offers products across multiple categories and serves customers throughout Brazil.

The business records every stage of the customer purchasing journey, including customer registration, product listings, order placement, payments, shipping, delivery, and post-purchase reviews. These operational activities generate large volumes of transactional data that can be used to evaluate business performance and support strategic decision-making.

---

# Business Environment (Simulated)

The simulated organization operates in a competitive e-commerce industry where customer expectations, product availability, delivery performance, and seller efficiency directly influence business success.

As transaction volumes continue to increase, management requires reliable reporting to understand overall business performance, monitor operational efficiency, and identify opportunities for growth. Access to accurate analytical information enables leadership to make informed decisions regarding sales performance, customer experience, seller management, and regional operations.

---

# Current Business Situation (Simulated)

The organization stores operational data across multiple relational datasets, including customers, orders, products, sellers, payments, reviews, and geographical information.

Although comprehensive transactional data is available, it is distributed across separate tables, making it difficult to obtain a consolidated view of business performance. Decision-makers currently lack a centralized reporting solution capable of providing integrated insights across different business functions.

As a result, business analysis requires significant manual effort, limiting the speed and effectiveness of strategic decision-making.

---

# Business Opportunity

The availability of historical transactional data provides an opportunity to develop a centralized analytics solution that transforms raw operational data into meaningful business information.

By integrating multiple datasets and presenting key business metrics through interactive dashboards, the organization can gain better visibility into sales trends, customer behavior, seller performance, delivery operations, and overall marketplace performance.

---

# Purpose of This Project

The purpose of this project is to demonstrate how an end-to-end data analytics solution can support business decision-making using publicly available retail data.

The project follows the CRISP-DM methodology and applies industry-standard data analytics practices, including data preparation, SQL analysis, exploratory data analysis, KPI development, dashboard creation, and business insight generation.

The outcome of the project is intended to showcase technical and analytical skills while simulating a real-world retail analytics engagement.

---

# Dataset Overview

This project uses the **Olist Brazilian E-commerce Public Dataset**, which contains historical e-commerce transaction data collected from a Brazilian online marketplace.

The dataset consists of nine related tables covering:

* Customers
* Orders
* Order Items
* Products
* Sellers
* Payments
* Customer Reviews
* Geolocation
* Product Category Translation

These datasets collectively represent the complete lifecycle of an online retail transaction, from purchase through delivery and customer feedback.
