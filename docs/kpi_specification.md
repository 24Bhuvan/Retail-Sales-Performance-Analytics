# KPI Specification

## Phase 13 — KPI Design

**Purpose:** Define the approved KPIs using consistent business definitions, formulas, source datasets, grains, filters, exclusions, aggregations, time bases, and business relevance.

**Scope:** KPI definitions only. Final KPI calculation is out of scope and begins in Phase 14.

---

## Calculation Standards

### Valid Orders
Unless a KPI explicitly states otherwise, order-level commercial KPIs use the project-defined revenue-eligible orders and exclude orders that are cancelled or unavailable.

### Revenue
**Revenue** means item revenue only:

`SUM(item_revenue)` or its validated order-level equivalent `SUM(order_revenue)`.

Freight is excluded from revenue and tracked separately.

### Multi-Item Orders
Multi-item orders must not be double counted when using order-grain KPIs. Item-level revenue may be summed, but order counts use `DISTINCT order_id`.

### Time Basis
Monthly sales KPIs use the purchase month (`year_month` derived from the order purchase date).

### Missing Values
Records with missing values required for a KPI are excluded from that KPI denominator unless the KPI definition explicitly states another rule.

### Payment Value
Payment value is a payment metric and is not interchangeable with revenue.

### Delivery Classification
Delivery KPIs requiring on-time or late classification use only classified delivered orders.

### Customer Identity
Customer KPIs use `customer_unique_id` for customer-level identity rather than transaction-specific `customer_id`.
